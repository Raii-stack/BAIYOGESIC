# HOPE landing page — Connected operations

Reference: https://www.shopify.com/ph (reviewed November 1, 2026).

## Design DNA

- Full-width photography establishes the business context before product detail.
- Navigation sits directly over the hero image, inside a wide shared content grid.
- Large, lightweight, tightly tracked headlines create hierarchy.
- White type and a dark image overlay keep the hero readable.
- Rounded primary and outlined secondary buttons form a clear action pair.
- A dark section with broad rounded top corners overlaps the hero.
- Large opening statements give way to smaller, denser product explanations.
- Muted secondary text and fine rules organize dark surfaces without repeated cards.

## Direction

**Thesis:** Make the workspace feel connected to the people and activity it supports.

**Preserved:** Photographic scale, restrained navigation, lightweight display type, rounded actions, and the curved transition into dark content.

**Pushed:** Replace the reference's changing commerce imagery with one original warehouse scene. Present HOPE's actual modules as a ruled editorial list, followed by a pale green team-access section.

**Why it works:** The hero establishes an operations context; the next section explains the existing capabilities; both entry actions lead to the established sign-in flow. No invented metrics, customer endorsements, or product capabilities.
